# Credits

## 3D models — “Toy Cartoon Planets”

- **Title:** Toy Cartoon Planets
- **Author:** [Gerhald](https://sketchfab.com/Gerhald)
- **Source:** https://skfb.ly/pCuq7 (Sketchfab)
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **File:** `public/models/toy_cartoon_planets.glb`

**Changes made in this project:** the original GLB file is unchanged. At runtime the app shows
one of the ten planets at a time: each is moved to the origin, rescaled (ringed planets smaller
so their rings fit), and given a lower emissive intensity, transparency for fading, custom
lighting, an atmosphere rim and orbiting particles.

The attribution is also shown on screen (bottom right) while the experience runs, with links
to the source, the author and the license.
